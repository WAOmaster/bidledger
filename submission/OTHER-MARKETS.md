# BidLedger: other spoken-deal markets

Scope: real settings where deals, quantities or commitments are spoken, multi-party, noisy and multilingual, and the written record is disputed. Sizes are from the sources cited; anything without a link is unverified and marked as such. Message counts are estimates (one utility template per party per settled deal), not Meta rate-card figures.

## Best 3 to pitch next

1. **Brazil construction diaristas.** Largest receipt count, WhatsApp is near-universal, and disputes are documented. Lead with the eSocial compliance pull. Blocker: LGPD consent.
2. **India fish landing auctions.** Huge volume and multilingual fit, and the ONDC and FMPIS work shows the government wants digital links. Blocker: buyer adoption.
3. **India cattle fairs.** Highest value per receipt, two receipts per head, and a state platform already shows demand. Blocker: farmer trust, which the clip addresses directly.
